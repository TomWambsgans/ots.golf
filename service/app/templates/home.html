How to play

  1. Fork the contract repository and edit one submission root: formal/Submissions/Lower/ or formal/Submissions/Upper/.
  2. Export the track's declarations with your claim in claim.txt, and check locally with verifier/verify.py.
  3. Open a pull request. The verifier fetches your commit, keeps only the submission root, and answers on the pull request. A verified claim that strictly beats the record is merged, and the merge is the promotion.

The rules are short. The repository is the source of truth: each submission root holds the current record. Scripts and agents: llms.txt.
